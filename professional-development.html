01Dry Dock Venture Studio HackathonVanderbilt · Spring 2026

1st Place OverallXtremis.AI Challenge Winner

Team
David Balin, Sam McFarland
Project
Spectrum pricing & liquidity model

A week with Xtremis building a market for underutilized wireless spectrum: a standard spectrum unit, short-term localized pricing, and a mechanism to bring liquidity and price discovery to a space that barely has either. The win sent the team to NYC Tech Week to pitch alongside Lorimer Ventures, with support from Cooley, Rho, HubSpot, and Microsoft Azure.

02VandyHacksVanderbilt · Fall 2025

Startup Launchpad WinnerBest Use of MongoDB

Team
David Balin, Sam McFarland
Project
OneTap

A digital wallet that recognizes checkout pages across 500+ retailers, runs vector search over your cards through MongoDB Atlas and Gemini, and surfaces the highest-reward card in under a second. Confirm and pay with one tap.

03Global Good HackathonVanderbilt · Spring 2026

Team

Project
FlowBreakr

An app that applies graph theory, linear algebra, and behavioral psychology to model addictive, pattern-like screen-time behavior and help users break it. Faculty are now working with the team on the underlying algorithm.
