01Dry Dock Venture Studio HackathonVanderbilt · Spring 2026

1st Place OverallXtremis.AI Challenge Winner

Team
David Balin, Sam McFarland
Project
Spectrum pricing & liquidity model
Prize
$45,000 overall, $5,000 Xtremis.AI Challenge, and $25,000 in Azure credits

A week with Xtremis building a market for underutilized wireless spectrum: a standard spectrum unit, short-term localized pricing, and a mechanism to bring liquidity and price discovery to a space that barely has either. The win sent the team to NYC Tech Week to pitch alongside Lorimer Ventures, with support from Cooley, Rho, HubSpot, and Microsoft Azure.

04VandyHacksVanderbilt · Spring 2026

Startup Launchpad WinnerBest Use of MongoDB

Team

05AWS Mission Autonomy HackathonFall 2025

$10K Prize Winner

Team
Kunal Bham
Project
Access911
Prize
$10,000

An AI voice agent that answers 911 calls when human dispatchers are overwhelmed, summarizes the key details, and routes them to a live dispatcher dashboard. Built on Twilio and ElevenLabs for the voice layer, with AWS Lambda, DynamoDB, and Elastic Load Balancing handling concurrent calls.
